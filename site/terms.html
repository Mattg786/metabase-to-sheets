Use

Use the add-on with a Metabase instance and Google Sheet you are authorised to access. You are responsible for the Metabase question, API key, spreadsheet, and any data you send through the service.

Licence

The free plan permits one saved sync configuration with manual sync and a Daily schedule. A verified Pro subscription permits the Hourly and Every 6 hours schedules and other limits stated on the pricing page. The current product does not promise unlimited syncs.

Paid terms

Price, billing interval, cancellation, refunds, and any taxes will be shown by Stripe and in the final approved paid terms. The pricing page shows Pro at $9 per month, USD, with a waitlist until sale opens. The site does not invite a live purchase until the payment link, legal text, and hosting are approved.

Dependencies

The add-on depends on Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, your Metabase host, Stripe for paid licence checks, and the selected hosting provider. A provider outage or an unreachable Metabase host can stop a scheduled sync. A failed sync leaves the previous synced cells in place.

Metabase to Sheets is offered by its developer. Google is not the seller of the add-on and does not provide the paid service.
